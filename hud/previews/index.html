To decide

  • One line per kill. Kill, headshot, wallbang and the rest are summed, the bonus is applied once, then rounded.
  • Colors. Normal XP is white. Boosted XP is gold with the old amount struck out and the multiplier chip, shown only when the bonus changed the amount (x1.3 on 1 XP rounds back to 1). Lost XP is small and red.
  • Like the toasts. Each line slides in from off the screen with a fade and blur, stays 3 seconds, and slides back out. New lines join at the bottom, 5 at most; when one leaves, the rest glide up.
  • Rank down is one quiet red line, no banner. Rank up gets the banner, placed under the MVP card's top slot.
  • Position. Right side by default, under the kill feed. Under the crosshair is the other option.
